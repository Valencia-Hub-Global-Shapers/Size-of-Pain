    How this map was made

    The outline and structures come from OpenStreetMap, where volunteers and memorial staff have traced the surviving remains, ruins and fences of each site. Every point is stored as a distance in metres from the camp's centre, then redrawn around wherever you place it, so the shape keeps its true size at any latitude instead of being stretched by the map projection.

    Area and perimeter: measured from the boundary outline drawn for this camp. Where the historical boundary is not mapped, the outline is the present memorial grounds, which can be smaller or larger than the camp was. Each camp's overview says which one you are looking at.

    Football pitches: a standard pitch is 105 m × 68 m (7,140 m²).

    Walking time: the perimeter at 5 km/h.

    Victim figures: taken from the memorial or museum responsible for each site, or from the scholarly estimates they cite. Many records were destroyed by the perpetrators, so most figures are estimates.
