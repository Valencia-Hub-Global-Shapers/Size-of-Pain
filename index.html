How this map was made

The overlay is based on documented site data from OpenStreetMap. Area, walking time, and familiar-unit comparisons are estimates designed to communicate physical scale.

Area: Derived from the documented perimeter of Auschwitz II–Birkenau. It represents the enclosed area within the BI and BII sectors.

Football pitches: A standard pitch is 105 m × 68 m (7,140 m²).

Walking time: Perimeter divided at 5 km/h.

Victim figure: Based on research by the Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial and Museum.

Building, fence, and boundary geometry: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

Basemap: CARTO / OpenStreetMap. Geocoding: Nominatim.

Historical context: Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial and Museum.
